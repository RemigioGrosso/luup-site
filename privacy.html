Luup

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Luup is a mobile app for finding parties in and around SUNY schools. This policy explains what information Luup collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account. When you open Luup, the app creates an anonymous account on your device so your votes, RSVPs and chat messages count once. It isn't tied to your name, email or phone number.
  • Your activity in the app: vibe votes, RSVPs ("I'm in"), chat messages you post, and messages you report or people you block.
  • Accounts (only if you sign up): your sign-in details (email address, phone number, username, or your Google or Apple account identifier, depending on how you sign up) and the nickname you choose. If you set up a host page: its name, colors and profile photo.
  • Basic technical data our hosting provider records to run the service, such as IP addresses in server logs, and crash reports when the app breaks.

Luup does not use your device's location, contacts or advertising identifier, and does not include advertising or analytics trackers.

What's public

You choose how each chat message appears. Posted anonymously, it shows as "Anonymous #" plus a number that's only used within that party, so your anonymous messages can't be linked across parties or back to you. Posted with your nickname, it shows your nickname, which other users can recognize across parties. Host names, colors and photos are public, since they appear on party listings and the leaderboard. Vote totals and RSVP counts are shown as numbers only.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show parties, count votes and RSVPs, and deliver chat.
  • To keep chat safe: filter banned words, act on reports, and apply blocks.
  • To let hosts sign in and manage their profile.

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Google provides the map, and Google sign-in if you choose it.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple if you choose it.
  • Sentry receives crash reports (device model, operating system and app version, and technical details of the error) so we can fix bugs. Crash reports don't include your name, email or messages.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. Reported chat messages may be hidden and kept so we can review them. When you delete your account, your nickname, host profile, photo, votes, RSVPs, messages, reports and blocks are deleted.

Your choices

  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account), or request deletion by email.
  • Block or report anyone in chat by pressing and holding their message.
  • Contact us any time to ask what we hold about you or to correct it.

Age

Luup is intended for people 18 and older and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has used Luup, contact us and we'll delete their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app.
