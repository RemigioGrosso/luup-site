Delete your Luup account

Last updated: October 11, 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Luup and tap the profile button at the top right of the home screen.
  2. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

By email

If you can't use the app, email support@luup.example from the email address on your account (or include the phone number or username you sign in with), with the subject "Delete my Luup account". We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your login and host profile, including your name, colors and photo
  • Your vibe votes and RSVPs (party totals update automatically)
  • Your chat messages, and the reports and blocks you made

Parties you hosted stay listed for the people who RSVP'd, but without your name. Server logs kept by our hosting provider are deleted on its normal schedule.

If you never signed up and only used Luup anonymously, uninstalling the app removes your access to that anonymous account. Email us if you'd like its votes and messages deleted too.
